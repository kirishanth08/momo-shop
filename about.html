🏮 The Story of Chopsticks

Born on Himalayan Ridges, Wok-Tossed for the City

Chopsticks started with a simple conviction: street food should never mean compromise. No frozen premade skins, no artificial chemical tenderizers, and no watered-down dips. Just honest flour, mountain aromatics, and roaring steam.

Taste the Craft Find the Stall
Who We Are

Authentic Himalayan Soul Meets Sizzling Street Flame

Founded right opposite Central Metro Exit 2, Chopsticks is a vibrant neighborhood street food stall built on respect for traditional Himalayan dumpling art and high-heat Indo-Chinese wok cooking.

Every morning at 6:00 AM, our dough is freshly mixed, hand-divided, and hand-pleated with secret herbs sourced directly from northeastern hill valleys. We never cut corners with frozen skins or industrial powders. Every plate is steamed or tossed to order in front of your eyes.

Street Milestones

Our 5-Year Journey

How a two-person dream on a humble wooden handcart grew into the metro's most loved evening pitstop.

1
Autumn 2021

The 2-Burner Handcart

Started on a modest wooden cart near the bus terminus, serving only two varieties: Darjeeling Steamed Veg & Classic Chicken with red stone-ground chutney.

2
Winter 2023

The Kurkure Crunch Breakthrough

After months of test-batching, we launched our signature cereal-dusted Kurkure Momos. Lines started wrapping around the corner within a week!

3
Spring 2024

Kiosk #14 & FSSAI Certification

Moved into a permanent modernized kiosk opposite Central Metro Exit 2 with commercial RO filtration, induction steamers, and thermal packaging.

4
Today (2026)

500,000+ Momos Served

Proudly feeding students, busy commuters, and neighborhood families with 28+ fresh menu offerings and fast 10-minute takeaways.

The People Behind the Steam

Meet the Stall Founders

The master artisans whose hands, recipes, and fire keep our counter buzzing every single evening.

Co-Founder & Head Momo Craftsman

Master Tashi Dorjee

14 Years of Himalayan Folding Mastery

Trained in the mountain kitchens of Kalimpong and Darjeeling, Master Tashi oversees dough resting, meat seasoning, and traditional hand-pinching. He crimps 15 distinct translucent pleats in under 4 seconds, locking all aromatic broth within the skin.

"If the dumpling dough isn’t translucent and tender enough to release its broth on the first bite, it has no place leaving my bamboo steamer."
🥟 Master Specialty: Darjeeling Paper-Thin Wrapper & Clear Herb Broth
Co-Founder & Master of the High Wok

Chef Arun Sharma

12 Years Street Flame Specialist

A high-flame street wok virtuoso who perfected the art of smoky "wok hei" breath across Kolkata and Delhi food alleys. Arun curates our signature chilli garlic noodle oils, crispy kurkure crust batters, and bubbling schezwan glazes.

"Real street food lives on high heat and intense timing. The wok must breathe fire so vegetables stay crisp and spices release their smoky soul."
🔥 Master Specialty: Burnt Garlic Wok Noodles & Kurkure Peri-Peri Crunch
Culinary Ethics

Our Speciality & Kitchen Values

What makes every bite at Chopsticks distinctly cleaner, juicier, and more satisfying than commercial machine-made snacks.

🥟

100% Hand-Rolled Dough

Rested for 45 minutes, hand-divided into walnut portions, and rolled with tapered edges so the pleats steam evenly without doughy clumps.

🌿

Pure Mountain Herbs

Crushed root ginger, scallions, toasted Sichuan pepper, and fresh coriander roots. Zero chemical flavor powders or artificial tenderizers.

🌱

Absolute Veg Isolation

Dedicated pure-veg bamboo steamers, separate woks, distinct color-coded tongs, and isolated chopping boards for uncompromising dietary purity.

🌶️

Daily Stone-Pot Chutney

Our signature red ghost pepper chutney is hand-crushed and stone-simmered every morning at dawn. Zero bottled vinegar preservatives.

💧

Multi-Stage RO Purity

All dumpling dough mixing, aromatic broth simmering, and dipping sauce preparation use commercial UV + RO filtered water certified safe.

📦

Eco Thermal Hot Packaging

100% unbleached kraft paper boxes that breathe gently so your fried momos stay crunchy and steamed momos retain their juicy broth on the journey.
