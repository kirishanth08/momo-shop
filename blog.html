Foodie Hacks

How Our Regulars Reheat Takeaways

Smart community tips for enjoying your hot box the next morning.

🥟 The Steamer Hack

Place cold momos inside an idli steamer or microwave with a wet paper towel for 45 seconds. Translucency fully restored!

🔥 The Air-Fryer Crisp

Air fry kurkure momos at 180°C for 3 minutes. The cereal crust becomes even crunchier than out of the fryer.

🍲 The Midnight Soup Drop

Drop leftover steamed dumplings into a bowl of instant ramen broth for instant restaurant-style wonton soup!
