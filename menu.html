Food Quality & Hygiene Standards

Customization & Allergen Advisories

We want your street meal to be completely safe, comfortable, and tailored to your tastes.

🌶️ Spice Customization

Every wok bowl and fried momo can be requested in Mild (gentle aromatic garlic), Medium (authentic street kick), or Inferno (ghost pepper glaze).

🌱 Pure Veg Steamers

We use strictly designated separate bamboo steamers, frying woks, and strainers for vegetarian items. No cross-contact with meats is guaranteed.

⚠️ Allergen Transparency

Contains wheat gluten (momos & noodles) and soy. Certain dips contain toasted sesame seeds. Please inform the counter if you have peanut or nut allergies.
